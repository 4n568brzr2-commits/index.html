Happy Birthday

I could have simply sent flowers.
But somehow, flowers alone didn’t feel like enough.

There are people we meet in life who become special to us in ways that are difficult to explain.

People whose happiness matters to us, even when we expect absolutely nothing from them.

You are one of those people.

Maybe you’ll wonder who sent these roses. Maybe you’ll have a few guesses. And maybe you’ll never know for sure.

That’s okay.

Because knowing who sent them isn’t the important part.

What matters is that today, somewhere, someone remembered your birthday and genuinely wanted to make you smile.

Someone wanted you to wake up and feel special. Someone wanted your day to be a little more beautiful.

Someone chose 100 roses because one somehow didn’t feel like enough.

Not because anything is expected in return.

Not a message.
Not a thank you.
Not even an answer to the question of who.

Your smile is enough.

So keep the mystery.

And whenever you look at these roses, don’t think about the person who sent them.

Think about yourself.

Think about how much life you still have ahead of you. How many beautiful places you haven’t seen yet. How many nights you haven’t laughed through yet. How many wonderful memories you haven’t made yet.

And whatever this next year brings you, I hope it brings you closer to the life that makes you truly happy.

You deserve to feel special today.

That is all these roses were ever meant to say.
Happy Birthday.
